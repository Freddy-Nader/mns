# Visual direction: monochrome, weight-driven, editorial lists

Four directions were sketched (Cartelera, Programa de mano, Índice, Luz y muro — see `sketches/`, kept on the "Direcciones" page of the design canvas). None was picked. Instead the client supplied reference sites, and those settle the question more precisely than the sketches did.

**The direction is: pure monochrome, carried by typographic weight and hairline editorial structure.** Named *Peso y lista* ("weight and list") for shorthand.

## References and what each one settles

- **davidsenra.com** — settles the hero and the bio. An enormous ultra-condensed black wordmark on a white ground, a single line of copy above it, a large photograph, black pill CTAs. The bio section is dark, centred, and reveals word by word on scroll.
- **theater.bamberg.de/termine** (ETA Hoffmann Theater) — settles the concert listing. Editorial serif month headings, hairline-separated rows, date at the left, black pill "Tickets" button at the right.
- **Resend / Vercel testimonial blocks** — settle the press mentions: a dark band, a large centred quote, small attribution beneath. Where those sites put company logos, this site puts publication names, because the proof here is critical reception, not clients.
- **Anthropic's footer** — settles the footer: a multi-column link grid, columns named for our audience segments (Asistir / Aprender / Contratar).

## Consequences

- **No chromatic accent.** Black, white, and greys only. Both primary references commit to this and it is what gives them their weight; adding a brand colour would dilute the one thing the direction is built on. Emphasis comes from size, weight and black fills. Revisit only with a specific reason.
- The direction is a hybrid of two sketches, not one of them: the *boldness* of Cartelera with the *editorial structure* of Programa de mano. Índice and Luz y muro are rejected.
- It satisfies the research constraint that the site must read as credible to a programmer and alive to a 22-year-old: the weight does the second job, the hairline listing structure does the first.
- High contrast throughout also serves the accessibility finding that concert audiences skew older.
- Client's explicit corrections to the Senra reference, which we implement rather than copy: **the hero photograph must be larger**, and the CTAs must not sit where Senra puts "Available everywhere you get podcasts" — they belong directly under the name.
