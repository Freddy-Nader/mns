# Imágenes — sources and rights

## The finding that matters

**mauricionader.net contains no photographs of him at all.** All 23 pages were crawled. The only images on his site are typeset programme sheets and two event posters.

For a performer whose selling point is the stage, that is the second-biggest content gap after the missing access data. **He needs a proper photo shoot**, or at minimum needs to put his existing press photos on his own site where they can be licensed and downloaded.

## ⚠️ Before this goes public

These are third-party press images used here to make a private mockup look real. **None of them is cleared for use on his own commercial site.**

- The Guanajuato file is published on a Mexican state government bulletin. Government publication is not a licence, and the photographer is not credited anywhere on the page.
- The *am.com.mx* and *Heraldo* images are newspaper photography and are certainly the outlets' copyright.

Before launch, each one either gets written permission, gets replaced with an image he owns, or comes out. Ask his office first — he will very likely have the original files and the photographer's name for the portrait, since it is a publicity image made for exactly this purpose.

## What a shoot should produce

Driven by the EPK conventions in the research — presenters need variety, not one image:
- A formal portrait, vertical and horizontal crops.
- A hands-at-the-keyboard detail.
- At least one live performance shot with an audience visible.
- One environmental shot with room around him for text overlay — posters and the hero need that space.
- Colour and black-and-white versions of each.
- Delivered at print resolution with the photographer's credit line attached, and a written licence covering web, print and third-party press use.
