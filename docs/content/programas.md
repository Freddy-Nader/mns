# Programas — his named concert concepts

Verified 2026-09-22. **Four of the ten names in the original research brief were wrong.** Corrections are in bold.

There are two families here and they should not be mixed: programmes with a permanent page on his site, and programmes that exist only in press releases.

## A. Standing touring programmes — each has its own page

| Nombre correcto | En el brief decía | Página |

- **pianoffortissimo** — lowercase, one word, the doubled "ff" set in bold italic as a play on the dynamic marking. Villa-Lobos, Grieg's Sonata Op. 7, six Rachmaninoff preludes plus the *Vocalise* in his own transcription, closing with Copland's *El Salón México* in Bernstein's transcription.
- **Humör Us, Piano** — umlaut on the *o*. ⚠️ His own site is inconsistent: the programme sheet prints "Humör Us, Piano" (ö in pink), the nav writes "HÜMOR US, PIANO" with the umlaut on the *U*. **We need to pick one.** Its own tagline is the description: *"It is Absolutely PERMITTED to laugh during the recital."* Saint-Saëns, P.D.Q. Bach, Dudley Moore's Beethoven parody, Hamelin's *Valse irritation d'après Nokia*, and his own *Four Parodies*.
- **Mexican Piano** — that's the shelf label; the concert title on the sheet is **"100 Years of Mexican Music"**. Ponce, Guraieb, Chávez, Ricardo Castro, Gustavo Morales, Velázquez, Ibarra, Zyman.
- **American Piano** — subtitled *"Music from the American Continent"*: pan-American, not US-only. Carrasco, Halffter, Chávez, Castro, Camacho, Nazareth, Gershwin, Muczynski, del Águila.
- **Don Quixote's Piano** — subtitled *"to Miguel de Cervantes Saavedra"*. Carbajo, Minkus in his own piano-solo version, Camacho, Lobato, Albéniz, Falla's *El retablo de Maese Pedro*.
- **MAKROpiano** — "MAKRO" caps, "piano" lowercase. Subtitled *Contemporary Music — Prepared Piano & Extended Techniques*. Cage, Lavista, Cowell, Crumb's *Makrokosmos*, Lucía Álvarez, Soto Millán, and his own *Imaginary silence*. Several works were written for the project, which ran 2012–2014 with FONCA support.
- **Piano & Orchestra** — not a programme but his concerto list: 28 concertos. Two annotations worth keeping on the site: works marked **\*** are ready to perform at any time, *including emergency replacements*; works marked **^** also exist in versions for piano & strings, piano & ensemble, or piano & chamber orchestra. **Both facts are directly sellable to a programmer** and are currently buried.

## B. Programmes confirmed in press, with no page of their own

- **Piano de Película** — his most active programme right now: four of the fourteen calendar dates. INBA's description: *"piezas creadas especialmente para películas que se han convertido en clásicos de la cinematografía, así como música icónica de concierto que ha acompañado a diversos filmes."* The 2026 edition is subtitled **"A 80 años del fallecimiento de Manuel de Falla"**. **It has no page on his site — the programme he plays most often is the one his site says least about.**
- **Música de cinco mundos** — Feb 2025, Festival Internacional Divertimento. Handel in his own adaptation, Grieg's *Holberg*, Khachaturian, the premiere of his *Toccata ostinata*.
- **Música de cuatro siglos** — Sept 2023, Festival Blanco y Negro, Cenart. His framing, quoted by INBA: he is drawn to *"repertorios que no fueron escritos para este instrumento."*
- **Sueño de amor / Amor sin barreras** — ⚠️ the full title is the pair, not just "Sueño de Amor". *Amor sin barreras* is the Spanish release title of *West Side Story*, which links this 2015 project to what became *Piano de Película*.
- **México nacionalista. 150 años de música para piano** — ⚠️ full title; the brief had only the second half. Jun 2019, Jardín Borda, Cuernavaca. Closes with his own piano-solo arrangement of Moncayo's *Huapango*.
- **Filosofía y Música** — **a programme nobody listed, and it's on the calendar twice this season, both free.** With the philosopher **Gerardo Martínez Cristerna**. Full title on the poster: *"La Enfermedad del Pensamiento y la Música como Esperanza."*
- **EntreDanzas** — Lavista and Arturo Márquez. Seen in an INBA headline only; description unverified.

## Why this section is worth building properly

Ten-plus packaged, repeatable, named concepts is an unusual asset and it is the thing a festival programmer actually buys. Right now they are buried under a nav item called "Repertory" and the most-performed one isn't there at all. The ETA Hoffmann poster grid is the right treatment. Out of scope for v1 — this is the next piece of work.
